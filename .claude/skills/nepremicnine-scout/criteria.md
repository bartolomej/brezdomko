# What we're looking for

Three friends looking to **rent** an apartment together in Ljubljana. Edit this file freely —
it's read fresh on every run. Hard requirements are filters; the rest are judgment calls.

## Hard requirements (reject if any fail)

- **Rental** (oddaja) of a whole apartment — not a single room ("Soba"), not a sale.
- **Location: Ljubljana city** (Ljubljana mesto). Reject anything in surrounding
  municipalities (Domžale, Medvode, Škofljica, Brezovica, Ig, Dobrova, Vodice, …).
- **3 separate bedrooms**, one per person. The room count on the card ("3-sobno",
  "4-sobno") counts the living room too, so it does NOT tell you the bedroom count:
  - Card triage: drop garsonjera, 1-, 1,5-, 2- and 2,5-sobno. For 3-sobno and up, fetch the
    listing and check "Št. spalnic" or the description for 3+ bedrooms.
  - A 3-sobno counts only if the listing clearly says 3 bedrooms (e.g. living room is
    combined with the kitchen and 3 rooms are separate bedrooms). A walk-through room or a
    living room "that could be used as a bedroom" doesn't count — mention it at most as a
    caveat on an otherwise strong listing.
- **Rent ≤ ~1.500 € / month.** Up to ~1.600 € is OK only if utilities (stroški) are
  included or it's otherwise outstanding. Lower is better.

## Nice to have

- Rent clearly below 1.500 € (say ≤ 1.300 €), or utilities included
- Good public transport to the center
- Furnished, or at least a kitchen that's fitted out
- Balcony, washing machine, separate toilet / 2 bathrooms
- Bedrooms of similar size (fair to split rent)

## Red flags (reject, or call out prominently)

- Landlord won't rent to students / groups of unrelated people / "samo družini"
- Short-term only, or long minimum term with big upfront payments beyond usual deposit
- Price far below market with no explanation (possible scam)

## How picky to be

Send every listing that meets all hard requirements — good rentals for 3 go fast, so
lean towards sending. Lead the reason with rent (+ utilities if stated) per person.
